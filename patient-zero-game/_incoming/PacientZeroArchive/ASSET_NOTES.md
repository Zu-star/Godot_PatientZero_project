# Asset notes

- Room, desk, and shelf source art comes from the three user-supplied PNGs in this conversation.
- Shelf base is cropped from the upper (unclicked) half of the supplied two-state shelf image. Its original embedded labels are covered by independently rendered cropped plates and native Godot labels.
- The rolled paper is cropped and polygon-masked for a separate transparent interactive sprite. Its generous invisible tap area is larger than its visible size.
- Elena and the framed photo are crops of the same approved desk art; the wide room intentionally has a smaller, simplified likeness.
- `shelf_original.png` is retained as an editing reference, not loaded at runtime.
- Other agent portraits and lockbox/card components are editable simplified SVG implementation art; they are deliberately cleaner than the room illustration and may receive a final art pass later. There are no extracted Photoshop layers or original layered source files.
- The paper sheet uses native text over a simple reusable PNG surface so clues remain crisp at different resolutions.
- No paid fonts, external audio, copyrighted stock packs or external Godot plugins were added. Audio files are simple locally synthesised placeholders.
- No generative image changes were made to the approved room and desk source images for this implementation.
