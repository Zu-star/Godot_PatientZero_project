# Developer solution / spoilers

1. Enter the left archive shelf close-up.
2. Tap the small rolled note beside the first box. Read the three filing rules.
3. Swap TRANSFERS and INCIDENTS: **DISPOSAL → INCIDENTS → STAFF → TRANSFERS**.
4. Tap the rightmost Transfers label or its box lid. The records and chained access kit appear together.
5. Open the records. The correct person is **Elena Morozova**, the dark-bobbed woman from the framed desk photo. Her ID is **7416**.
6. Close the records, tap the chained box, enter **7416**, press Unlock.
7. Tap the access card inside the opened kit. It is automatically selected after pickup.
8. Back → Back returns to the room. Tap the quarantine reader to unlock the door.
9. Tap the door again to complete the level / load the configured next scene.

The desk can be inspected before or after the shelf puzzle. Viewing the photo or reading the note is not a forced progression flag: players who deduce or remember a clue can proceed. The correct code alone cannot bypass the unsolved shelf. Incorrect code entry never resets other progress.

Unique solution check: Transfers is slot 4. Incidents/Staff must be slots 1–2 or 2–3. Slots 1–2 would put Disposal in slot 3 adjacent to Staff, violating rule 3. Therefore Disposal/Incidents/Staff occupy slots 1–3 respectively.

The fictional extra personnel names and IDs, interface, physical box art and sound placeholders are new implementation details. They can be changed without touching the approved room backgrounds.
